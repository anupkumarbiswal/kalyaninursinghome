Our Story

Kalyani Nursing Home was established in 2005 with a single, unwavering purpose — to make quality healthcare accessible to every citizen of Angul and its surrounding regions. Located on Bypass Road, Gandhi Marg, Bazarpada, we started as a small clinic and have since grown into a fully-equipped nursing home trusted by thousands of families.

Over the past two decades, we have served more than 5,000 patients, delivered hundreds of babies safely, and supported countless families through difficult health journeys. Our growth is a testament to the trust our community places in us.

Today, Kalyani Nursing Home is staffed by a dedicated team of qualified doctors, experienced nurses, and compassionate support staff who believe that healing goes beyond medicine — it begins with a caring touch.

🎯 Our Mission

To deliver accessible, affordable and high-quality healthcare services to every patient we serve, with integrity and compassion at the core of everything we do.

🔭 Our Vision

To be the most trusted healthcare provider in Angul district — known for clinical excellence, patient safety, and a culture of genuine care.
