How to Find Us

Conveniently located in the heart of Angul

🚌

Regular bus services stop at Angul Bus Stand. Take a local auto-rickshaw to Gandhi Marg, Bazarpada — approximately 10 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries

Do you accept walk-in patients? +

Yes, we accept walk-in patients at all times. For non-emergency consultations, you may also book an appointment in advance to avoid waiting time.

Is there a 24-hour emergency service? +

Absolutely. Our emergency ward is operational 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Call +91 67657 96393 immediately in an emergency.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We accept cash, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), NEFT/IMPS bank transfers, and most major insurance schemes. Please speak to our billing team for details.

Do you provide ambulance services? +

Yes, we have ambulance facilities available. Call our number for immediate ambulance assistance and our team will coordinate accordingly.
